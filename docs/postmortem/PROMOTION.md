# Promotion Assessment

## 1. What Worked

1. A clear live URL, quick-start documentation, and printable one-page onboarding material make first contact straightforward.
2. The project narrative (creative control plus guided iteration) is understandable and differentiable.

## 2. What Is Missing

1. No formal promotion pipeline (content calendar, launch channels, social proof strategy, creator partnerships, or KPI dashboard).
2. Limited evidence of conversion tracking from interest to active usage.

## 3. Net Result

1. Product communication is clear at the documentation level, but growth and distribution remain early-stage and mostly ad hoc.
